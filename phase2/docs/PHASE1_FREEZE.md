# Phase I freeze

Phase I is historical exploratory and replication work and is not modified by Phase II.

- Frozen commit: `feceba1fabbc8ba74d0bc55ca0ed6317a3a44bf0`
- Frozen branch: `archive/phase1-v0.6`
- Phase II working branch: `agent/chile-phase2-foundation`

The frozen Phase I contains the original English and Chilean Spanish studies, institutional framing, institution-prestige mapping, focused academic replication, and hidden-metadata academic review. Its strongest positive result was a surname-to-institution-prestige association. Its larger decision replications did not show stable elite-surname decision leakage.

Phase II was designed after those results and therefore treats Phase I as hypothesis-generating. Phase II prompts, model panel, providers, primary estimands, and analysis plan are frozen before confirmatory outcomes are collected.

No historical Phase I data, scripts, or result files are moved into the Phase II tree. The exact commit above is the archival reference.
